How does 50:50 work?

Your first use in each run is free and removes two incorrect answers from the current question. After that free use, you can earn a second use on a later question in the same run by watching a rewarded ad. You can use 50:50 no more than twice per run. Both uses preserve your eligibility to score 100/100.

What is One More Chance?

One More Chance is available once per run after your first wrong answer. Choosing Accept Defeat ends the run. If a rewarded ad reports that you earned the reward, you can retry the same question; watching the ad does not award the answer or a point. Answer the retry correctly to earn the point and continue the run with 100/100 still achievable. A wrong retry ends the run. Cancelling the ad, an ad failure, or an ad that does not report an earned reward does not consume your One More Chance allowance.

What kinds of ads does the game use?

The game has no banner ads, interstitial ads or forced gameplay ads. Rewarded ads are started only when you choose to use them for the second 50:50 or One More Chance.

An ad will not load or I did not receive a reward

Check your internet connection and retry when the app indicates that an ad is ready. Ads may be unavailable. Closing an ad before the reward is earned does not grant the second 50:50 or One More Chance, and does not consume that allowance. If the problem continues, email support@archmorrow.com.

Where can I change advertising privacy choices?

On HOME, look for Privacy Options. This control appears when Google’s consent system reports that privacy options are required, so it may not appear for every user. Read the privacy policy for details about Google’s consent flow and data processing.

Where are my scores saved?

Your Personal Best, Total Runs, Total Correct Answers, trophy unlock and settings are saved locally on your device. There is no account or login, Archmorrow backend, in-app cloud sync or cloud leaderboard. Avoid deleting the app to troubleshoot unless you are prepared to lose its local records.
